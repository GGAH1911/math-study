## 문제

$$\int_{-2}^{a} f(x)\,dx = \int_{-2}^{0} f(x)\,dx$$

(1) 16 $\quad$ (2) 14 $\quad$ (3) 12 $\quad$ (4) 10 $\quad$ (5) 8
