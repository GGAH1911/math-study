    - "**$\\angle FDC=2\\angle DCF$ 조건.** $\\triangle DCF$에서 $\\angle DCF=\\varphi$로 놓으면 $DC=l$, $DF=2$, $CF=r=\\tfrac{2s}{3}$. 사인 법칙 적용: $\\dfrac{2}{\\sin\\varphi}=\\dfrac{l}{\\sin 3\\varphi}\\Rightarrow l=8\\cos^2\\varphi-2$, $\\dfrac{2}{\\sin\\varphi}=\\dfrac{2s/3}{\\sin 2\\varphi}\\Rightarrow\\cos\\varphi=\\dfrac{s}{6}$. 대입하면 $l=\\dfrac{2s^2}{9}-2$, 즉 $s^2=\\dfrac{9(l+2)}{2}$ …(Ⅱ)"
