  steps:
    - "**좌표 설정**: 원의 중심 $C=(0,0)$, 반지름 $n$. $AB=2$이므로 $A=(-1,-\\sqrt{n^2-1})$, $B=(1,-\\sqrt{n^2-1})$로 놓으면 $|CA|=|CB|=n$, $|AB|=2$ 성립."
    - "**∠BAC 계산**: $\\vec{AB}=(2,0)$, $\\vec{AC}=(1,\\sqrt{n^2-1})$이므로 $\\cos(\\angle BAC)=\\dfrac{\\vec{AB}\\cdot\\vec{AC}}{|AB||AC|}=\\dfrac{2}{2n}=\\dfrac{1}{n}$."
    - "**점 D 결정**: 각의 이등분 방향 = $\\hat{u}_{AB}+\\hat{u}_{AC}=(1,0)+\\frac{1}{n}(1,\\sqrt{n^2-1})\\propto(n+1,\\sqrt{n^2-1})$. 직선 $A+t(n+1,\\sqrt{n^2-1})$를 원 방정식에 대입하면 $t=0$ (점 $A$)과 $t=1$ (점 $D$)를 얻어 $D=(n,0)$."
    - "**BD 계산**: $BD=\\sqrt{(n-1)^2+(n^2-1)}=\\sqrt{2n(n-1)}$."
    - "**점 E 결정**: $E=(n\\cos\\theta_E,n\\sin\\theta_E)$, 호 $AD$ (B 미포함, 상반부)에 놓임. $BD:DE=\\sqrt{2}:1$이므로 $DE=\\sqrt{n(n-1)}$. 현의 길이 공식 $DE=2n\\sin(\\theta_E/2)$에서 $\\sin(\\theta_E/2)=\\dfrac{\\sqrt{n-1}}{2\\sqrt{n}}$. 제1사분면 조건으로 $\\cos(\\theta_E/2)=\\sqrt{\\dfrac{3n+1}{4n}}$을 선택하면 $\\cos\\theta_E=\\dfrac{n+1}{2n}$, $\\sin\\theta_E=\\dfrac{\\sqrt{(n-1)(3n+1)}}{2n}$이므로 $E=\\left(\\dfrac{n+1}{2},\\dfrac{\\sqrt{(n-1)(3n+1)}}{2}\\right)$."
    - "**검증**: $|CE|^2=\\dfrac{(n+1)^2+(n-1)(3n+1)}{4}=\\dfrac{4n^2}{4}=n^2$ ✓, $DE^2=\\dfrac{(n-1)^2+(n-1)(3n+1)}{4}=n(n-1)$, $BD:DE=\\sqrt{2}:1$ ✓."
    - "**$S_n$ 계산**: $S_n=\\dfrac{1}{2}\\left|\\det\\begin{pmatrix}n&0\\\\\\frac{n+1}{2}&\\frac{\\sqrt{(n-1)(3n+1)}}{2}\\end{pmatrix}\\right|=\\dfrac{n\\sqrt{(n-1)(3n+1)}}{4}$."
    - "**극한 계산**: $\\dfrac{S_n}{n}=\\dfrac{\\sqrt{(n-1)(3n+1)}}{4}=\\dfrac{\\sqrt{3n^2-2n-1}}{4}$이므로\n$$\\frac{\\sqrt{3}}{4}n-\\frac{S_n}{n}=\\frac{\\sqrt{3}n-\\sqrt{3n^2-2n-1}}{4}=\\frac{2n+1}{4\\left(\\sqrt{3}n+\\sqrt{3n^2-2n-1}\\right)}$$\n분자·분모를 $n$으로 나누면 $\\to\\dfrac{2}{4\\cdot 2\\sqrt{3}}=\\dfrac{1}{4\\sqrt{3}}=\\dfrac{\\sqrt{3}}{12}$."
    - "**$p,q$ 결정**: $\\dfrac{q}{p}\\sqrt{3}=\\dfrac{\\sqrt{3}}{12}$이므로 $\\dfrac{q}{p}=\\dfrac{1}{12}$. $p=12$, $q=1$ (서로소 자연수). $\\therefore p+q=\\mathbf{13}$."
