    - "**정확한 삼각값 계산:** $\\sin^2\\alpha=\\tfrac{119}{128},\\;\\cos\\alpha=\\tfrac{3}{8\\sqrt{2}}$; $\\;\\sin\\theta_{AB}=\\tfrac{2}{R},\\;\\cos\\theta_{AB}=\\tfrac{29}{8\\sqrt{15}}$; $\\;\\sin\\theta_{CD}=\\tfrac{4}{R},\\;\\cos\\theta_{CD}=\\tfrac{11}{4\\sqrt{15}}$."
    - "**$\\sin\\beta$ 계산:** $\\beta=\\pi-\\theta_{AB}-\\alpha-\\theta_{CD}$이므로 $\\sin\\beta=\\sin(\\theta_{AB}+\\alpha+\\theta_{CD})$. 단계적으로 가산법 적용: $\\sin(\\theta_{AB}+\\alpha)=\\tfrac{\\sqrt{119}}{2\\sqrt{30}},\\;\\cos(\\theta_{AB}+\\alpha)=\\tfrac{-1}{2\\sqrt{30}}$. 이어서 $\\sin\\beta=\\tfrac{\\sqrt{119}}{2\\sqrt{30}}\\cdot\\tfrac{11}{4\\sqrt{15}}+\\tfrac{-1}{2\\sqrt{30}}\\cdot\\tfrac{\\sqrt{119}}{4\\sqrt{15}}=\\dfrac{10\\sqrt{119}}{8\\sqrt{450}}=\\dfrac{\\sqrt{238}}{24}$."
